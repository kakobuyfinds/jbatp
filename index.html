What to check before ordering

  1. Compare measurements

    Measure a similar garment you own and compare its chest width, shoulder width and length against the seller’s size chart. For shoes, check the chart against your foot length.

  2. Check the exact variant

    Use the item ID to return to the listing and confirm the color, size and seller. If your agent offers warehouse QC photos, check these details again before approving shipment.

  3. Estimate the delivered cost

    Add the item price, domestic delivery, agent fees and international shipping. Check whether the shipping line charges by actual or volumetric weight and whether taxes apply.

Frequently asked questions

What is a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping shortlist that helps you compare listings by category, price and source. The entries on this page open their product pages on HipobuyFind.

Can I use these listings with LoveGoBuy?

Open the HipobuyFind product page and look for the original marketplace link. If it is available, check whether LoveGoBuy accepts that link and supports the exact variant you want.

Are the listed prices live?

The table shows a catalog snapshot. Open the source listing for current prices and availability, and check the final cost with your chosen shopping agent before paying.
